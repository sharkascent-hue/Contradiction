Do Quranic stories about Jesus and the Seven Sleepers come from earlier Christian legends?

Several Quranic stories match texts outside the Bible. Jesus making clay birds (3:49, 5:110) appears in the Infancy Gospel of Thomas, which scholars date to the mid-to-late 2nd century and in which the five-year-old Jesus makes twelve clay sparrows and brings them to life.
