Do Matthew and Luke give contradictory genealogies of Jesus?

Matthew says Joseph's father was Jacob and traces the line through Solomon son of David (Matthew 1:16). Luke says Joseph was the son of Heli and traces it through Nathan son of David (Luke 3:23). The two lists also differ greatly in length between David and Jesus. Al-Hindi's catalogue of Gospel disc

The discrepancy is real and was recognized by early Christians themselves. It counts against a strict, word-for-word inerrancy view of the Gospels. Whether that view is a Christian necessity is disputed among Christians, and the argument does little against Christians who accept the Gospels as theologically shaped history. The harmonizations are possible but cannot be proven.
