Did Judas hang himself (Matthew 27:5) or fall and burst open (Acts 1:18)?

Matthew says Judas threw the silver into the temple and 'went away and hanged himself', and the priests used the money to buy the potter's field (Matthew 27:5-7). Acts says Judas himself 'obtained a field' with his reward and, 'falling headlong, his body burst open, and all his intestines gushed out

This is one of the stronger 'contradiction' examples, because the accounts differ on several specific points, not only on emphasis. Harmonization is logically possible but adds details neither text gives. Most critical scholars (Ehrman, and many non-inerrantist Christians) treat them as two independent traditions.
