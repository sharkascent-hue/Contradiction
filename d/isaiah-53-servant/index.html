Is the suffering servant of Isaiah 53 the nation of Israel or the Messiah?

Singer argues that Isaiah 52:13 to 53:12 is the fourth of the Servant Songs, and the earlier songs name the servant explicitly as Israel/Jacob (41:8-9, 44:1, 44:21, 45:4, 49:3). The speakers in chapter 53 are the astonished 'kings' of 52:15, who in the messianic age confess that they despised Israel

Both sides can cite early Jewish evidence, so neither 'the messianic reading is a Christian invention' nor 'the national reading is a medieval reaction to Christianity' holds up. The national reading has the strongest support from the surrounding chapters; the individual reading has the strongest support from 53:8-9, where the servant suffers for 'my people'.
